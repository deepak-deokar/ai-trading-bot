## Findings

- Largest gaps are market holidays (Christmas, New Year, Good Friday), not data faults.
- February–July 2023 is thin (66–84% of normal bars): HistData feed holes. Strategy tests skip any day missing minutes inside its trading window.
- Ticks stepping back ≤2 seconds (feed jitter, mostly 2019+) are kept at the latest seen time; larger backward steps are rejected (~0.007% of ticks).
- 2014–2018 spreads are almost constant (0.35–0.51): that feed quoted fixed spreads, so it understates spread spikes. The expensive cost profile adds extra spread on top of every quote.
- Spreads widened in 2025–2026 (median ~0.6) as gold rose above $3,000–5,000.
- 1-minute moves over 1% cluster at 13:30 UTC (US data releases) and Sunday opens, plus the January–February 2026 sell-off: real market moves.
