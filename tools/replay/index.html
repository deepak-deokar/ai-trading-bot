Prices are the mid of recorded bid/ask minute data (HistData), grouped into candles. Fills: a buy pays half the spread above the mid plus $0.05 slippage, a sell the same below; exits pay it again. A candle that touches both the stop and the target counts as the stop. Every open trade is closed at 16:45 New York, so nothing is held overnight. Only 2014–2022 days are used; 2023 onward stays reserved for testing the bot.
